Colour

Dark grounds, with colour used like bioluminescence: sparingly and glowing. Aqua leads for Deepdive. Gold and felt green are the casino's additions. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

Type

Wordmark and headings

Deepdive

Manrope ExtraBold (800), tracking −3.5%. In the logos the letters are outlines, so they look the same everywhere.

Casino sub-mark

CASINO

Manrope ExtraBold, all caps, tracking +42%, in gold. It reads like the lettering on a casino sign.

Body

Seven prompts. The rarer your answer, the deeper you sink.

Manrope Regular to SemiBold (400–600), 16px and up on screens, line height 1.5.

Using the logos

Do
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as the jellyfish's bell.
  • Use the dark version on deep navy or black, and the light version on white or pale grey.
  • Use the one-colour white mark on photos, felt or busy backgrounds.
  • Keep the mark at least 24px tall on screens. Below that, use the favicon.
Don't
  • Recolour the jellyfish or swap the gold for another colour.
  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add drop shadows to the logo.
  • Rebuild the wordmark in another font.
  • Put the casino chip in the Deepdive logo, or the bare jellyfish in the casino's.
