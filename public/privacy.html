Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

What's stored on your device

The game keeps these in your browser's local storage so they're there next time you visit. They never leave your device unless noted below.

  • Your pearl balance, daily chest streak and Pearl Dive count.
  • The name you choose for live tables.
  • Your settings (sound, music, effects) and your place in solo games.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it, including your pearls.

What our server sees

When you play at a live table, our server receives your table name, your moves, the bets you place and your pearl balance, so it can run the game.
